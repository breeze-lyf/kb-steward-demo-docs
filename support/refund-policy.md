## Refund window
Customers may request a full refund within **30 days** of purchase.

## Partial refunds
After the refund window, a partial refund (store credit) may be offered at the support lead's discretion.

## How to issue a refund
Issue refunds from the billing console under *Orders → Refund*. Refunds above $500 need a second approver.
