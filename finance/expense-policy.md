Example data. Applies to all Northwind Example Co. employees.

## Meal allowance
Effective July, when travelling for work, the daily meal allowance is **$65 per day**. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Receipts
Attach an itemised receipt for every expense above **$40**, except that, effective July, meal expenses require receipts for amounts above **$25**. Card slips alone are not accepted.

## Submitting a claim
Submit claims in the expense tool within **30 days** of the trip. Your manager approves first, then finance.

## Hotels
Book hotels through the travel portal. The nightly cap is $180 in tier-1 cities and $130 elsewhere.
