## Allowance
Full-time employees get 25 days of paid time off per year, plus public holidays.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year and expire on March 31.

## Requesting time off
Request PTO in the HR system at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.
